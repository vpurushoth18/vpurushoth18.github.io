01 / ABOUT

The short
version.

My research asks what a model keeps when a signal gets noisy, a speaker changes, or the language is underrepresented. I’ve explored this through speech embeddings, radio signals, and multimodal systems.

Before UNR, I studied electrical and electronics engineering at NIT Tiruchirappalli and built a speech assistant for tea graders at Pekoe.ai. In summer 2026, I studied open-set radio signal recognition at Idaho National Laboratory.
